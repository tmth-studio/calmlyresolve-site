About

A small company, run in the open.

Calmly Resolve is a venture of Tough Minds Tender Hearts Ltd, a company registered in England. Everything this site says traces to a versioned design record and a published valuation standard.

The founders

Tom Manuel and Clare Manuel founded this venture in 2020, as The Justice Community. Clare Manuel is a solicitor with over 30 years in the legal industry. Its first model took claims on directly, one claimant at a time, and processed over £400,000 of disputes in its first year. That taught us the direct route does not reach enough people. Clare Manuel stepped down in 2026, and Tom Manuel now runs the venture through Tough Minds Tender Hearts Ltd.

How the venture is run

Calmly is built to run with an AI operator, Dex. That is how a small company can offer cover on millions of transactions at a fee that stays small. Dex runs the first conversation with a site, the benchmark and the trial protocol. Tom Manuel is the accountable owner and joins at the point of terms. Terms, prices, signatures and spend are his and nobody else's.

The valuation method is a published, versioned standard. The venture's design is held in a versioned record, and every claim on this site maps to a line in it.
