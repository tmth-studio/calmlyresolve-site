What you pay

And what you do not.

You pay
  • One fee per covered transaction, monthly in arrears, on your own transaction count.
  • A floor while the trial runs. The published rate applies only once the trial shows the cover has paid for itself.
You do not pay
  • Nothing per dispute. The fee does not vary with dispute volume.
  • No capital, no reserve, no fund. No claim is ever presented to you for payment.
  • Nothing for your participants. They pay nothing at any stage.

The rate and its floor are set out in the written agreement, with the arithmetic behind each one. What you pay, in full.

What we do not ask
  • No exclusivity, in either direction. Either side can stop on a month's notice.
  • No confidential data before the written agreement.
  • No integration commitment before the benchmark.
  • No belief in an uplift figure. There is none for your site yet, which is why the first months are a trial.

The standard

Every valuation is produced under a published, versioned standard.

Each report states the version it was produced under. Both parties to a dispute receive the same report at the same time. Changes go through change control, and no unversioned method values a claim. The record of what the method predicted against what happened will be published from the first trial. An independent accountant checks it under agreed-upon procedures. No institution countersigns an individual report.
