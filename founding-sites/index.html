A measured start

Cover on every transaction your site forms. A participant your own process has refused gets a credible exit at a valued price. You pay a small fee per covered transaction and nothing when a dispute happens. The start is a measurement, not a promise.

Cover sits behind your transactions from the moment each one forms. A dispute your own process has ended in refusal is valued under a published method and pursued. Your process stays yours.

You pay one fee per covered transaction, monthly in arrears. It starts at a floor while the network readout runs. It moves to the published rate only once measured value shows the cover has paid for itself. Nothing per dispute. No exclusivity. No capital. Either side can stop on a month's notice.

Who this is for

Marketplaces and services platforms in the UK that earn about £25 or more on each transaction, from commission, take rate or a booking fee. Below that level the arithmetic does not carry at launch, and we would rather say so here than three meetings in. The cover takes three kinds of dispute: marketplace goods; site services and money owed; deposits and holdings.

What your site does

  1. Place the cover line at the moment a transaction forms. One sentence from an approved set; your legal team reviews it once.
  2. Route a dispute only at final refusal. One flag at the exit of your own dispute process. Nothing routes earlier, and we take no part while your process runs.
  3. Pass the records for that dispute, with the participant's authorisation: the transaction, the contract terms and the refusal history. The participant gathers nothing.
  4. Send your monthly transaction count. That count is the meter. It is reconciled against your records each month.
  5. Carry the split for the readout. During the readout window, transactions are assigned at random to a covered arm and an uncovered arm. The participant does not see the split. After the readout, every transaction is covered.

What we do not ask

  • No payment per dispute, ever. The fee does not vary with dispute volume.
  • No exclusivity, in either direction.
  • No capital, no reserve, no fund. You take no claim risk and no dispute risk.
  • No confidential data before heads of terms.
  • No belief in an uplift figure. There is none for your site yet, which is why the start is a measurement.

What your participants get

The wording at the point of transaction, with your site's name in place of "the site":

This transaction carries cover from Calmly Resolve. The site pays for it; you pay nothing. If a dispute ends in the site's final refusal, an independent method values your claim. Calmly's pool takes your claim on and pursues it at that value; you are paid from what it recovers. Once a committed buyer exists, you can sell the claim at its valued price.

Stated at purchase, so no participant finds it out later. The valuation report is the participant's to read and to keep. We never send you per-claim data. You receive counts and outcomes in aggregate, never an individual claim.

What is routed and valued, but not taken on

  • A claim against a party in insolvency, dissolution or strike-off when the dispute arises. We check the public registers when the dispute arrives.
  • A claim below £2,500. It is still valued, and the report is still the participant's to keep. The minimum is an assumption the first site's intake will test.
  • A claim outside the three kinds above. Financial services, credit, insurance and personal injury claims are out.
  • At launch, a claim against a sole trader or an individual. Only claims against trading companies are taken on until the first readout.
  • Until committed money stands behind the cover, the cover is conditional, and the wording says so.

What you pay

  • Until the readout: the floor. A fee per covered transaction, billed monthly in arrears on your own count. It is our cost of carrying the cover, disclosed with its arithmetic. Not a discount, and not a price.
  • From the readout: the published rate. A percentage of your revenue per transaction, with a lower and an upper limit per transaction. Your revenue per transaction is read once a year from your published take rate and average transaction value, or attested from your accounts.
  • Warranted downward only. The rate steps up only when the readout shows measured value at or above it. If your numbers carry less, the rate falls to what they carry. Forward only, with no back-charge. Nothing can raise the rate above the published card. The floor period ends at twelve months at most.

No figure is published on this page. The floor, the percentage and the limits are set out in the heads of terms, with the arithmetic behind each one. Every parameter is an assumption until the readout replaces it.

The network readout

One readout runs across all live founding sites, not one per site. It falls at the earlier of a pooled transaction count set in the readout protocol or twelve months from the first site's go-live. We measure, from our own records, the disputes routed, admitted, valued and taken on, their outcomes and the time to each. We measure, from your records, conversion and average transaction value in the covered arm against the uncovered arm. We also measure the refund and goodwill spend on disputes your process could not close. Your side runs under a protocol agreed in writing before it starts. You see your raw result, its standard error, the network figure, the weight on your own result and your warranted figure, with the arithmetic. Then the rate decision: the published rate, or a lower rate set from your warranted figure.

Either side can stop

You can stop on one month's notice at any time. If the readout does not show the cover paying for itself, the rate never steps up. We can end the paid arrangement if submitted disputes exceed the share set in the heads of terms. We can also end it if measured value comes in below the floor. Either way, disputes already routed are completed, and every transaction covered while you were with us stays covered.
