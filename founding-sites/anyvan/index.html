AnyVan and Calmly Resolve: a measured start

Cover sits behind AnyVan's transactions from the moment each one forms. A participant your own process has refused gets a credible exit at a valued price. Your process stays yours.

You pay one small fee per covered transaction, monthly in arrears: a floor while we measure, the published rate only once the network's measured uplift, weighted with your own, shows the cover has paid for itself, never more than your warranted figure carries. Nothing per dispute. No exclusivity. No capital. Either side can stop on a month's notice.

1 · What AnyVan does

  1. Place the cover line at transaction formation. One sentence from an approved set; your legal team reviews it once.
  2. Route a dispute only at final refusal. One flag in your dispute-flow exit state. Nothing routes earlier.
  3. Pass the records for that dispute, with the participant's authorisation: transaction, contract terms, refusal history. The participant gathers nothing.
  4. Send your monthly transaction count. That count is the meter, reconciled against your records each month.
  5. Carry the split for the readout. During the window, transactions are assigned at random to a covered and an uncovered arm; the participant does not see it. After the readout every transaction is covered. Calmly expects two to six weeks from signature to first covered transaction.

2 · What we do not ask

  • No payment per dispute, ever. The fee does not vary with dispute volume.
  • No exclusivity, in either direction.
  • No capital, no reserve, no fund. You take no claim risk and no dispute risk.
  • No confidential data before heads of terms.
  • No belief in an uplift figure. There is none for AnyVan yet, which is why the start is a measurement.

3 · The pivot either side can call

AnyVan can stop on one month's notice at any time. If the readout does not show the cover paying for itself, the rate never steps up.

Calmly can end the paid arrangement if submitted disputes exceed 0.15% of covered transactions, or measured value comes in below £0.50 per covered transaction. Either way, disputes already routed are completed.

4 · What your participants get

The wording at the point of transaction (61 words, launch form):

This transaction carries cover from Calmly Resolve. AnyVan pays for it; you pay nothing. If a dispute ends in AnyVan's final refusal, an independent method values your claim. Calmly's pool takes your claim on and pursues it at that value; you are paid from what it recovers. Once a committed buyer exists, you can sell the claim at its valued price.

Stated at purchase, so no participant finds it out later. At AnyVan the claim is typically damage to goods in transit, goods lost, a move not completed as booked; the other party is the removal provider. Many trade as limited companies; many are sole traders. Where AnyVan is itself the contracting party, the claim is against AnyVan.

  • A claim against a party in insolvency, dissolution or strike-off is not taken on. Calmly checks the public registers when the dispute arrives.
  • A claim below £2,500 is routed and valued but not taken on. The valuation report is still the participant's to keep.
  • Three kinds of claim are in: marketplace goods; site services and money owed; deposits and holdings. Financial services, credit, insurance and personal injury claims are out.
  • At launch, only claims against trading companies are taken on. A claim against a sole trader or an individual is routed and valued but not taken on.
  • The cover, the claims and the court route are England and Wales. A dispute whose claim would be issued in another country is not taken on.
  • Until committed money stands behind the cover, the cover is conditional, and the wording says so.

5 · What you pay

  • Until your readout: the floor. £0.30 per covered transaction, billed monthly in arrears on your own count. That is Calmly's cost of carrying the cover, disclosed with its arithmetic. Not a discount; not a price.
  • From your readout: the published rate. 1.25% of your revenue per transaction. Never below £0.31, our cost floor. Never above the most optimistic scenario for AnyVan: the value the cover would produce for you if the assumptions hold, which the trial tests. At your revenue per transaction that upper figure is £1.12 and the rate you would pay is £0.64 (band £0.38 at r £30 to £0.90 at r £72). Your revenue per transaction is read once a year from your published take rate and average transaction value, or attested from your accounts.
  • Warranted downward only. The step up happens only when your readout shows measured value at or above the rate. If your numbers carry less, the rate falls to what they carry. Forward only, no back-charge. Nothing can raise the rate above the card. The floor period ends at twelve months at most.

6 · The first readout: the network readout

One readout across all live launch sites. It falls at the earlier of 215,000 pooled completed transactions across live sites or twelve months from the first site's go-live. Expected inside six months of AnyVan's go-live; AnyVan is warranted almost entirely on its own numbers.

What Calmly measuresWhat AnyVan sees
From Calmly's records: disputes routed, admitted, valued, taken on; outcomes and time to outcome; submitted disputes as a share of covered transactions. Counts and outcomes in aggregate. Never an individual claim.
From your records: job conversion and average job value, covered against uncovered; refund and goodwill spend on claims your process could not close. The same figures by arm, on your own data, under a protocol agreed in writing before it runs.
Measured value per covered transaction (uplift × your revenue per transaction, plus the tail spend that fell away) against the published rate. Your raw result, its standard error, the network figure, the weight on your own result, and your warranted figure, with the arithmetic. Then the rate decision: the card rate, or a lower rate set from your warranted figure.
